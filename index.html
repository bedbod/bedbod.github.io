bennet de boer design

I’m Bennet de Boer, a designer & drag artist living in Hamburg.
I’m currently pursuing a bachelors degree in communication design at HAW Hamburg.

My work consists mostly of editorial, photo & video projects with a focus on identity, queerness, costume & fetish.
I’m interested in combining confrontational, deconstructive, or conceptual ideas with a systematic & parametric approach, balancing order and chaos.
