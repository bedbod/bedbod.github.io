MOTION IS DESCRIBED IN PHYSICS AS THE CHANGE IN POSITION OF AN OBJECT OR BODY REGARDING A REFERENCE POINT OVER A GIVEN SPAN OF TIME.  IT IS MATHEMATICALLY DEFINED IN TERMS OF DISPLACEMENT, DISTANCE, VELOCITY, ACCELERATION AND FRAME OF REFERENCE TO AN OBSERVER.

THE STUDY OF THE GEOMETRICAL ASPECTS OF A PHYSICAL OBJECT'S MOTION INDEPENDENT OF THE FORCES THAT SET THEM IN MOTION IS CALLED KINEMATICS.

EVERY MOVEMENT CAN BE DIVIDED INTO TWO FUNDAMENTAL CLASSES. TRANSLATION AND ROTATION.

TRANSLATION DEFINES THE PARALLEL, SIMULTANEOUS DISPLACEMENT OF ALL AN OBJECT'S POINTS.
A BODY IN THREE-DIMENSIONAL SPACE HAS ACCESS TO THREE DEGREES OF FREEDOM IN TRANSLATION.
THE X-, Y- AND Z- DIRECTION.

LEFT AND RIGHT, UP AND DOWN, FORWARD AND BACKWARD.

THE BODY’S EXPANSION ALONG THESE AXES ARE DEFINED AS WIDTH, HEIGHT AND DEPTH.

ROTATION DEFINES THE CIRCULAR SHIFT OF EVERY POINT OF AN OBJECT AROUND A COMMON AXIS.
THE MOTION OF A BODY ALONG THE THREE ROTATIONAL AXES ARE NAMED ROLL FOR THE LONGITUDINAL AXIS, PITCH OR NICK FOR THE TRANSVERSE AXIS, AND YAW FOR THE VERTICAL AXIS.

EVERY PHYSICAL PROCESS IS INTERTWINED WITH THE TRANSMISSION OR TRANSFORMATION OF ENERGY.

the call of your nature and the voice of god

2025/2026 | Three-Dimensional Design | Sculpture/Set Design [c. 67 × 50 × 28 cm] | Mixed Media

With his “structural model of the psyche” Sigmund Freud divides the mind into three agencies: the id, the ego and the super-ego.

The id represents our inherent drives and needs, while the super-ego stands for society’s laws and values. The ego is the active agency, which needs to find a compromise between the two opposing demands.

I’m interested in the conflict of an “inner” and “outer” voice in situations, in which a person must decide between instinctive lust and godly commandments, and the emotions accompanying this collision.
This sculpture/ set design model seeks to visualise Freud’s model and show the torn state of mind, of someone acting sexually in a way, that is despised by their religious belief.
However, which room stands for religion or sexuality, id or super-ego is designed to be ambiguous.

This project was constructed for the course “Inszenierte Räume”, supervised by Prof. Natalia Stachon.

#101 – M/M:M+M

[Man or Machine on Music and Memories]

2025/2026 | Editorial Design, Photography | 7 Booklets [loop-stitch binding] & Slipcase [30 × 30 × 3cm] incl. 101 Photos | InDesign, Illustrator, Lightroom Classic, stats.fm

Music is omnipresent and highly personal. Every person’s taste in music is as unique as themself and listening to music evokes the memory of familiar places or long gone times.

Streaming services have become the dominant way to engage with music in the twenty-first century. Spotify records every minute you listen to and every song you stream, resulting in a large, mostly hidden database of personal music listening habits.
Additionally, every song uploaded to the streaming service gets automatically algorithmically analysed on its Audio Features.
Songs get assigned values for commonly used characteristics like tempo, runtime and key as well as other—more subjective—categories like Danceability, Instrumentalness or Energy.

Unlike our human perception of music, songs don’t get tied to personal unique memories by the algorithm but strings of values and numbers, which try to estimate, predict and categorise the song as objectively as possible regardless of any context outside the uploaded audio file.
This process turns ephemeral musical moments into clear, objective data and the subjective perception of songs into unwavering values.

#101 seeks to give these concealed algorithms a physical form and then to examine if disembodied values can infer the makeup of a person's biography.
For the book I analysed and visualised my personal Spotify data collected from the 18th of October 2022 until the 30th of March 2024.

Every song is represented by an image. A mostly abstract photo taken by me to visually share my subjective perception of the musical mood, textures, sound and theme of the song.
Additionally, every song is assigned an “objective” code and a corresponding symbol. The code is comprised of a song's algorithmically generated Audio Feature values. The symbol is an interpretation of this string of numbers and is composed of seven variable glyphs, which correspond to the seven Audio Feature attributes and change appearance on a sliding scale based on its underlying value.
The Song Symbol and Song Image are overlaid to form a Song Cover meant to combine and contrast the subjective and objective way of translating musical attributes and visualising a song.

This project was developed in the course “Bilder der Meinen”, supervised by Prof. Stefan Stefanescu.

FURIOS #34 – weder noch

2026 | Editorial Design | Magazine [21,0 × 29,7 cm] | Analog, InDesign

WEBSITE: https://furios-campus.de

I was asked to design the layout and cover for issue #34 of FURIOS, the campus magazine at the Freie Universität Berlin.
The theme of the issue is „weder noch“ [“neither nor”].

For the visual identity, I experimented with ways to represent grey areas, opposites and middle grounds through typographic means. The text boxes switch between justified, left- and right alignment, creating a ragged shape emphasising the literal middle ground of the page. Elements span diagonally across the page and unusual white spaces are left behind.

The layout was designed digitally without color, but through analog illustrations, which were drawn on top of the text-only pages and later scanned, the magazine simultaneously stays serious & minimal, while becoming expressively colourful & youthfully kitsch.
The cover, which can function as a separate poster, shows a mixed media chimera; an animal which is neither fully one nor another.

SILBER

2025 | Time-Based Media | Video [03:00 min], 1 Mask | DaVinci Resolve, Super 8 Film, Mixed Media

SILBER [silver] is a music video shot entirely on Super 8 film using a Braun Nizo 561 Macro camera, loaned to us by Analogfilmwerke e.V.
The video was filmed in collaboration with Leontine Fruck and stars Julius Faustmann.

It follows a man in an empty, dream-like space, who begins to have visions of busy crowds. These visions trigger the appearance of a fish, with which he gradually begins to merge. This reinterpretation of classical mermaid stories stands as a metaphor for anxiety and agoraphobia.

Franz Kafka’s “Metamorphosis” and David Lynch’s “Eraserhead” serve as inspiration for the story and visual identity. The distortions and crossfades were exclusively done in camera, using hair gel on a glass pane in reference to Man Ray’s “L´Étoile de Mer”. We shot on one Kodak Tri-X film [03:20 min] and developed it in the HAW’s photo lab.
The song was created specifically for the video by Julius Faustmann and David Kuhl.

This film was made during the course “MUSIKVIDEO GOES SUPER 8”, supervised by Prof. Katharina Duve.

[Dis]Play the System

2025 | Spatial Design, Typography | Exhibition | InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop, Artivive, Mixed Media

WEBSITE: https://www.play-the-system.xyz/x/en

[Dis]Play the System translates “play the system.xyz”—an archive of over 400 student projects on rule-based, parametric design, collected by Heike Grebin during nearly two decades of teaching at HAW Hamburg—into a physical space and makes it tangible. Five websites, one book, and this exhibition all draw from the same database, demonstrating the multimedial ways content can be designed and conveyed.
The exhibition was held from the 18th to the 20th July 2025 in KULTUR & GUT, a café and cultural space in Altona, Hamburg.

The planning of this exhibition brought together twenty design students with various backgrounds. Together, we sought to exhibit not just works from the archive but the archive itself, displaying the huge number of works within it and the large bandwidth of formats, approaches, themes and topics in parametric design equally.
Using AR, the exhibition was made interactive, while information stayed engaging, simple but also space-saving, respecting the pre-existing design of the space. The exhibition is made to look archival, structured and organised while interdisciplinary, fun and inviting.

The visual identity and use of numbers as a structural element to encode information is loaned from the website, which itself is transformed into a dim room at the back of the space, projected with recordings of the various sites, representing all 382 projects within the archive.
The works are sorted into groups by medium, while large images on twin-wall sheets additionally portray the exhibited books photographed prior in KULTUR & GUT. These, along the exhibited posters, can be scanned and animated using Artivive, causing a repetition across different formats and continuing the theme of multimedial design.

This exhibition was created during the course “[Dis]Play the System”, supervised by Prof. Heike Grebin and Katharina Wanke.

VOLTO

2024/2025 | Time-Based Media | Video [03:13 min] | DaVinci Resolve, GarageBand, Touchdesigner

„Wir sehen, was wir nicht sehen“ [“We see, what we don’t see”] was our prompt for the creation of a three-minute short film.
I was initially inspired by noses, which are constantly in our filed of view but rarely noticed. However, the nose is the only part of our face that we can see without the use of mirrors or photographs. This thought sparked the realisation that you only ever see representations of your face but are unable to perceive it yourself like others do.
If you do not truly know what you look like, how does that inform your view of your own body and appearance?

This short film is an experimental self portrait portraying my thoughts and questions following this prompt. The video touches on body dysmorphia, ugliness, the perception of others, stereotypes and identity.
It is divided into four chapters: I don’t know what I look like; What do I look like?; Who do I look like? and Who am I?

To represent an inability to perceive and to invoke discomfort, I experimented freely both practically and digitally with various different methods of abstraction and cut the results together to form the different chapters without an internal storyline to form a digitally distorted & associative self portrait.

VOLTO refers to an outdated Italian word for face but also voltage as a reference to the glitches and electrical interference.

This video was made in the course “Zeitbezogene Medien Basis”, supervised by Prof. Katharina Duve.

VIVIDWHALE

2024 | Type Design | Variable & Modular Glyph-System, Posters | Glyphs, InDesign

Instead of consisting of singular glyphs, these modular shapes are meant to be combined to form an unlimited set of different letters.
The typeface and accompanying posters are inspired by Hito Steyerl’s “How Not to be Seen: A Fucking Didactic Educational .MOV File”, which deals with invisibility, surveillance and the transference of digital objects into reality.

The film features 1950’s resolution targets, which have been neglected and are now cracking. The combination of shapes with organic and geometric qualities was the base for the typeface and acts as a metaphor for the merging of the real and the digital. The actual letters become invisible in the wild convergence of different forms and hide in plain sight.

The variable font consists of three differently styled subgroups, whose weight can be individually adjusted. The names of these groups reference types of whales.
Additionally, a VIVIDWHALE, the first word I could write while developing the font, is ironically something hard to hide.

This typeface was created during the course “Type Design Basis”, supervised by Prof. Pierre Pané-Farré.

mm

2024 | Editorial Design, Typography | Booklet [16 × 24 mm] | InDesign

The inspiration for this booklet is Karl Gerstner’s “Programme entwerfen”, which describes a parametric approach to design–creating systems or rules are set to design automatically.

The layout of “mm” is identical on nearly every page. Every textbox has a defined font-size and line height and is linked automatically to the following one. Texts on design theory are then inserted in these textboxes after another without adjusting the layout to the text’s length or meaning. This system gradually and autonomously creates a shift between visual hierarchy and importance of content.

This project was created during the course “Typografie Basis”, supervised by Prof. Heike Grebin.
